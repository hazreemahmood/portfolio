How to read these numbers

Gmail evidence was last reviewed on 8 Nov 2026. This dashboard includes applications with submission evidence from 6 Aug 2026 onward. Duplicate receipts for the same role, such as Fuku’s Indeed and Workable confirmations, count once. CS Malaysia’s two differently titled applications count separately.

“Further response” includes decisions, next-step requests, assessment feedback, and a portal-message notification. Automated receipts, job advertisements, candidate-experience surveys, and general recruiter registration forms do not count. Next-step applications overlap with the latest-status categories: JXING and ARBA later rejected the application.

Two additional rejections arrived in this period—EverestEngineering and AirAsia Software Engineer I—but their submission receipts were not found in this period. They are outside the submission-period group. The earlier 13 figure counted response emails, not applications.

No Gmail update does not prove an employer never replied. Phone, WhatsApp, job portals, and other accounts may contain additional responses. PERSOL’s August candidate-registration form is not treated as a response to its September Team Lead application. An hourly local task checks whether today’s Gmail review is complete. It refreshes this page at most once per Malaysia calendar day and pushes changes through the existing CI/CD pipeline. This page does not access Gmail in your browser. The Mac must be awake with the app running for that scheduled review. Waiting times are measured as of 8 Nov 2026.
