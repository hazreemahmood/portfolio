How to read these numbers

This is a static snapshot of Gmail evidence reviewed for 6 August–5 November 2026. The 31-role group includes applications with submission evidence in that period. Duplicate receipts for the same role, such as Fuku’s Indeed and Workable confirmations, count once. CS Malaysia’s two differently titled applications count separately.

“Further response” includes decisions, next-step requests, assessment feedback, and a portal-message notification. Automated receipts, job advertisements, candidate-experience surveys, and general recruiter registration forms do not count. The four next-step applications overlap with the latest-status categories: JXING and ARBA later rejected the application.

Two additional rejections arrived in this period—EverestEngineering and AirAsia Software Engineer I—but their submission receipts were not found in this period. They are outside the 31-role group. The earlier 13 figure counted response emails, not applications.

No Gmail update does not prove an employer never replied. Phone, WhatsApp, job portals, and other accounts may contain additional responses. PERSOL’s August candidate-registration form is not treated as a response to its September Team Lead application. This page does not sync with Gmail. All waiting times are measured as of 5 November 2026.
